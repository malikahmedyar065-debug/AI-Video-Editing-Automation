AI Video Editing Automation

An open-source project exploring AI-powered video editing automation to make professional video production faster, easier, and more accessible.

🎯 Project Vision

The goal is to develop an intelligent video editing system that automates repetitive editing tasks and simplifies the process of turning raw footage into polished videos.

The project is aimed at independent content creators, YouTubers, social media creators, and small businesses.

🚀 Planned Features

- Automatic Video Analysis: Analyze footage and identify useful scenes.
- Smart Scene Selection: Help identify the most relevant moments.
- Automated Cutting: Remove unnecessary footage and pauses.
- AI Subtitles: Generate and synchronize subtitles.
- Audio Processing: Improve audio clarity and synchronization.
- Short-Form Video Creation: Prepare videos for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.
- Export Automation: Simplify video rendering and output.

🛠️ Planned Technologies

- Python
- FFmpeg
- OpenAI Whisper
- AI-assisted editing workflows
- Automation tools

Technology choices may evolve during development.

💡 Problem We're Solving

Traditional video editing can require significant time, technical knowledge, and repetitive manual work.

This project aims to reduce those barriers through accessible automation while maintaining control over the final creative output.
